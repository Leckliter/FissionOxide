### P0s found

- **Heads-up input Tab:** in a rectangle, Tab doesn't move from width to height, so `40 Tab 20
  Enter` gives a 20-wide rectangle with one dimension.
- **Forwarded file not drawn:** a file passed to the running instance opens, but its body isn't
  drawn until a visibility toggle.

### Other notable gaps

- **No camera animation:** every camera change snaps instantly.
- **ViewCube:** only the 6 faces are clickable; no edge or corner hot spots, no rotate or roll
  arrows.
- **Marking menu:** the overflow list covers the ring when right-clicking mid-canvas.
- **Edge double-click:** selects one edge, not its loop.
- **Timeline:** the marker can't be dragged and features can't be reordered.
- **Sketch colours:** fully constrained curves don't turn black.
- **Phantom hub saves:** 117 empty "Untitled" designs were saved into the hub without the user
  saving.
